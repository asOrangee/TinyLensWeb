# Use the Stage-A predicted image family in Stage-B review

Stage A will produce one immutable Stage-A Predicted Image Family from its accepted posterior-median mass and source model. Stage B and M0–M3 will consume that same family instead of independently recomputing it, so the topology reviewed during mask construction is identical to the topology used by downstream position likelihoods.

The required Stage-A artifact will be a machine-readable JSON document containing image IDs assigned after deterministic `(x, y)` coordinate sorting, image count, image-plane coordinates, the position tolerance, and sufficient posterior-median and solver provenance to reproduce its derivation without exposing the Stage-A pickle as an expert interface. Its provenance contains exactly the mass parameters, mass centre, shear, source centre, solver name and configuration, position tolerance, minimum log likelihood, Stage-A attempt reference, and image-plane coordinate convention needed to reproduce the family. Failure to produce at least two finite, valid image-plane positions is a Stage-A operational Hard Gate failure; the run never falls back to the older evidence path.

Mechanical coverage is determined only from the image pixel whose centre is nearest the predicted position. Pixel centres follow `x = (column - (nx - 1)/2) * DPIX` and `y = (row - (ny - 1)/2) * DPIX`; an exact distance tie selects the smaller row or column index, and an out-of-bounds prediction invalidates the Stage-A artifact.

Every Stage-B attempt will provide required `diagnostics/stage_b_morphology_audit.png` and `artifacts/stage_b_morphology_audit.json` artifacts that align the predicted positions with the current lens-light-subtracted S/N map and Arc Mask, alongside the existing Stage-A model and Stage-B diagnostics. A fixed `0.3 arcsec` neighbourhood, owned by the Pipeline Release and not tunable during a Lens Run, supplies local peak and above-threshold S/N evidence, pre-filter `threshold_island_id` values and sizes, filtering survival, post-filter `candidate_component_id` and selected-component identity, nearest-mask distance, and local coverage information. Threshold Island IDs are a separate namespace and are never valid Arc Component Selection targets. The combined diagnostic will initially overlay predicted positions without adding a separate Stage-A model-plane FITS artifact.

The Stage-B Expert response removes the overlapping free-form `coverage_findings` and `uncertainties` fields and instead requires one `predicted_image_dispositions` entry for every and only the stable image IDs, each carrying one of `covered_consistent`, `supported_uncovered`, `unsupported_prediction`, or `indeterminate` plus cited evidence. `covered_consistent` must agree with mechanical coverage, and `supported_uncovered` must agree with mechanical non-coverage. Acceptance requires every entry to be `covered_consistent`; a parameter or selection retry requires at least one `supported_uncovered` and permits no unsupported or indeterminate entry; an uncertain outcome requires at least one `unsupported_prediction` or `indeterminate`.

The expert may repair `supported_uncovered` through either one authorized mask-parameter change or one replacement complete Arc Component Selection, according to the demonstrated cause; those repairs remain mutually exclusive. If no credible single repair exists, or if a prediction is unsupported or indeterminate, the lens routes to Human Review rather than being silently accepted.

Stage Adjudication derives the Human Review reason from the per-image dispositions: any `unsupported_prediction` produces `reason_kind: scientific_blocker`; otherwise an `indeterminate` produces `reason_kind: scientific_uncertainty`. If both occur, the blocker classification takes precedence while every per-image finding remains preserved.

The morphology-audit JSON records, for each image, its ID and position, nearest pixel and coverage, nearest selected-arc distance, local peak S/N and peak position, local above-threshold pixel count, each intersecting Threshold Island's global and local size, peak S/N and small-island-filter survival, candidate and selected component IDs, and local selected-arc pixel count and fraction. Top-level provenance records the family reference, current mask parameters, `DPIX`, the `0.3 arcsec` evidence radius, and mask convention. The PNG contains one global S/N, selected-mask and predicted-family view plus a dynamically sized `0.6 arcsec` square cutout for every predicted image, with no omitted images.

Family validation requires at least two finite, in-bounds positions separated pairwise by at least the solver's `cluster_tol=0.08 arcsec` and deliberately imposes no maximum image count.

Predicted positions remain model-derived evidence rather than independent detections, so they never create mask pixels automatically. This avoids both observed faint-image loss from purely local connected-component filtering and circularly forcing an unsupported Stage-A prediction into the downstream imaging data.

The Packaged Pipeline will own one local Arc Mask Construction module that returns both the final mask and the complete intermediate morphology trace used by the audit. Stage B will consume that returned mask rather than generate a second trace beside the external black-box result; numerical equivalence tests against the previous `arc_mask_from` behavior protect unchanged parameter semantics. The default mask parameters, including `arc_ignor_size=5`, remain unchanged and all lens-specific repair continues through the existing Stage-B retry contract.

The Stage-B Review Context embeds validated copies and Lens-Result-relative paths for the accepted `stage_a_predicted_image_family.v1` record and the current `stage_b_morphology_audit.v1` record, supplies the accepted `stage_a_model.png` path, and binds the audit to the family with SHA-256. Both schemas reject unknown fields. The misleading `effective_missed_*` names are replaced by `large_uncovered_threshold_islands`, pixels, and fraction, while Stage-B metrics also report the predicted-image count and nearest-pixel uncovered count; these remain evidence rather than scientific Hard Gate thresholds.

The Stage-A Review Context also exposes the predicted family so the Stage-A Expert must compare its image count and positions with `stage_a_model.png` during the existing topology review, but Stage A gains no second per-image disposition contract. Structured Predicted-Image Coverage Dispositions remain solely the Stage-B Expert's responsibility.

`large_uncovered_threshold_islands` uses the current attempt's `arc_ignor_size` as its area reference rather than a second fixed threshold. `local_selected_arc_fraction` is the selected-arc pixel count divided by all pixels inside the `0.3 arcsec` neighbourhood that are not excluded by the Modeling Mask; the audit records both numerator and denominator.

This changes the Stage-A artifact contract, Stage-B review evidence, and Stage-B acceptance contract and therefore uses Pipeline Release `stage-a-image-family-mask-audit.v1`. The unchanged configuration fields retain schema `lens_pipeline_config.v4`. Existing Lens Runs remain immutable and are neither backfilled nor reviewed through the older fallback path; comparison of an existing result requires a Full Lens Rerun under the new release.

Regression coverage includes Stage-A family generation, sorting and provenance; invalid-family Hard Gate behavior; the synthetic 3-pixel plus 4-pixel faint-image filtering failure; strict per-image response and refinement validation; and downstream consumption of the saved family without rerunning the position solver. The real Euclid lens remains a read-only integration check rather than a dependency of the fast test suite.
