# Arc Mask Expert owns Stage-B scientific acceptance

> Stage-B's specialist scientific authority remains, but its read-only role and
> orchestrator-owned execution are superseded by
> [ADR 0018](0018-stage-experts-and-deterministic-adjudication.md).

Once the deterministic Hard Gate passes, the read-only Arc Mask Expert is the sole scientific authority for accepting, refining, or routing a Stage-B arc mask to human review. Stage B does not receive an additional generic reviewer and decision-maker pass because it produces the mask the specialist already evaluates; the Lens Orchestrator retains exclusive authority to apply proposed lens-local changes and rerun Stage B, preserving auditability and separation between scientific judgment and mutation.
